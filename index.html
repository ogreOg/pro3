Who We Are

Three Decades of Casting Excellence

Vertex Diecast Pvt. Ltd. is one of India's leading manufacturers of precision aluminium and zinc die-cast components. From our state-of-the-art facility in Pune, Maharashtra, we serve automotive, electrical, and industrial manufacturers across the globe — combining deep metallurgical expertise with modern automation.

Every component that leaves our foundry carries the weight of rigorous quality control, decades of process refinement, and a commitment to sustainable manufacturing.

  • ✓ In-house tool & die design and fabrication
  • ✓ Fully automated high-pressure die casting cells
  • ✓ CNC machining, surface finishing & assembly
  • ✓ Lab-tested metallurgical certification on every batch

What We Do

End-to-End Die Casting Solutions

01

High-Pressure Die Casting

HPDC cells from 250T to 1200T producing thin-walled, high-strength aluminium and zinc components at scale with tight tolerances.

02

Gravity Die Casting

Permanent mould gravity casting for medium-to-high volume parts requiring superior mechanical properties and dense structure.

03

CNC Machining

Multi-axis CNC machining centres deliver precision post-cast finishing, threading, and milling to exact specifications.

04

Tool & Die Making

In-house design and fabrication of dies and moulds, reducing lead times and keeping full control over quality.

05

Surface Finishing

Powder coating, anodising, plating, shot blasting and vibratory finishing for components ready for final assembly.

06

Quality & Testing

Spectrometers, X-ray inspection, pressure testing and dimensional CMM checks on every production run.

How It Works

From Concept to Component

1

Design & Simulation

Our engineers collaborate with your team to optimise part geometry for castability, using flow and thermal simulation.

2

Tooling & Die Build

Dies are designed, fabricated and tested in-house — cutting weeks off typical lead times.

3

Melting & Casting

Certified alloy ingots are melted, degassed and poured or injected under tightly controlled parameters.

4

Machining & Finishing

Castings move to CNC, deburring and surface treatment lines to reach final dimensional and aesthetic spec.

5

Inspection & Delivery

Every batch is inspected, documented and packed for dispatch — with full traceability.
